Shipping Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

We are committed to delivering your order as quickly and safely as possible. Below you will find details about our shipping methods and estimated delivery times.

Standard Shipping (Free)

Estimated delivery: 7–12 business days

Available on all orders within the United States. Tracking information is provided via email once your order ships.

Express Shipping

Estimated delivery: 3–5 business days

Available for an additional fee at checkout. Subject to availability based on your location.

Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days. Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed the next business day.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive an email with a tracking number. Please allow 24–48 hours for tracking information to update after shipment.

International Shipping

At this time, we ship within the United States only. We are working to expand our shipping capabilities to additional countries in the near future.

Shipping Issues

If your order has not arrived within the estimated delivery window, or if you experience any issues with your shipment, please contact us through our Contact Page and we will resolve the issue promptly.
